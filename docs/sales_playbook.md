# Atellica CI: territory sales playbook (Delhi NCR and Bengaluru)

**Product facts used (public sources):** Atellica CI is Siemens Healthineers' integrated clinical chemistry and
immunoassay analyser: 200+ parameters across 20 disease states, critical tests in about 14 minutes (vs 22–40
conventionally), microvolume sampling that helps paediatric and low-volume samples, and lower water and plastic
use. It was first deployed in India at Mahajan Imaging & Labs (2024). Since June 2026, Atellica chemistry reagents
are also made locally at Siemens Healthineers' Vadodara plant.

## Who to call first (from the account model)
1. **Hospitals with analysers 7+ years old and an immunoassay-heavy menu** (oncology, fertility, maternal
   screening): the biggest value and the strongest reason to switch.
2. **Accounts on local or refurbished analysers**: lower switching cost; pitch reliability and accuracy.
3. **Existing Siemens accounts due for replacement**: protect and upgrade the base before a competitor bids.
4. **Lab-chain branches**: one win can open the chain; target the chain's central purchasing team.

## Pitch by segment
| Segment | Their problem | Atellica CI message | Proof to bring |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Large hospital | Emergency turnaround, many tests, space | Critical results in ~14 min; one integrated analyser for chemistry + immunoassay | Turnaround time study on their own test mix |
| Fertility / maternal / oncology labs | Specialised immunoassays (AMH, maternal markers, tumour markers) | Wide immunoassay menu incl. AMH and maternal screening markers | Reference site visit (Mahajan Imaging) |
| Lab chain branch | Standardisation across sites, reagent supply | Same platform across branches; locally made chemistry reagents | Chain-wide cost-per-test model |
| Standalone lab | Budget, sample volumes | Microvolume sampling, compact footprint; financing options | Cost-per-test vs current setup |

## Timing
Run demos about 3 months before each account's budget month. Tier A accounts cluster in Nov–Mar
(see `output/budget_calendar.csv`).

## What to measure each quarter
Demos per rep, demo → quote and quote → win rates by segment, win value, and the competitor named in each loss.

Sources: BioSpectrum India (June 2026) on local manufacturing of Atellica chemistry reagents in Vadodara;
